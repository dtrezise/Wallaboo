# Financial Model Notes

The Excel model is an illustrative five-year operating model for 2027–2031 with Conservative, Base, and Upside cases.

Key conventions:

- Currency: U.S. dollars; model display is primarily whole dollars.
- Revenue excludes sales tax and customer-paid shipping.
- Kickstarter/platform and payment costs are modeled at 9% of DTC product revenue.
- Landed cost includes product, normal inbound freight, duty/tariff reserve, receiving, and quality loss.
- Founder compensation is shown separately from pre-compensation EBITDA.
- Crowdfunding receipts are treated as product sales, not financing.
- The $75,000 seed reserve is a planning liquidity buffer released by milestones.
- No debt, interest, income tax, or working-capital timing schedule is modeled because there is no operating history or confirmed production calendar.
- Every hardcoded input is blue; same-workbook links are green; calculations are black.

The model is not a valuation and should be re-based after a flagship bill of materials, supplier quotes, fulfillment quotes, and prelaunch conversion data exist.
