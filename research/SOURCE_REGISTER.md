## Research cautions

- Games/Puzzles category totals include trading cards, puzzles, mass-market games, and licensed products; they are a ceiling, not a serviceable market estimate for Mr Wallaboo.
- The 2024 Kickstarter success rate describes projects published on the platform, including established creators and very small campaigns. It is not a probability forecast for a first-time Wallaboo campaign.
- Manufacturing figures are public budgetary examples, not quotes for a Wallaboo bill of materials.
- Import duties are unusually fluid. The model uses a landed-cost contingency instead of claiming a permanent tariff rate; every purchase order requires a fresh customs and supplier check.
- The name search performed for this study is preliminary internet research, not a legal clearance opinion.
